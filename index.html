Terms and Privacy Policy

1. For all ages. MediMate has no minimum age limit. Children, teenagers and users aged 18 and younger may use it only with a parent or legal guardian who sets up or supervises the profile. A child must not manage or take medicine based only on this app.

2. Not medical advice. MediMate is a reminder and record-keeping tool. It does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend medicines, calculate doses, check drug interactions or replace a doctor or pharmacist. Use only medicines prescribed or approved for that person by a qualified healthcare professional. Never start, stop, change, share or take an extra dose because of this app or a streak record.

3. Adult supervision for children. For users aged 18 and younger, a parent or guardian must check the medicine name, strength, dose, timing, food instructions and duration against the current prescription or label, and supervise medicine-taking. Do not guess unclear instructions. Ask the prescribing doctor or pharmacist to clarify them. Only mark a dose as taken after confirming it was actually taken as directed.

4. Loud alerts are not guaranteed. MediMate can play a repeating sound and spoken reminder while the app is running, the page is open and the browser allows audio. The actual loudness depends on the device volume, silent/do-not-disturb mode, connected headphones or speakers, browser permissions, battery and operating-system settings. Browsers may pause or stop timers and audio when a tab is backgrounded, the device is locked, the browser is closed, the device sleeps or the device is offline. Notifications and calendar alerts can also be delayed, blocked or fail. No alert can be guaranteed to be heard or delivered. Test the sound, keep device volume and permissions appropriate, and use a separate reliable alarm or caregiver check for important medicines. Never rely on MediMate as the only reminder, emergency system or medical device.

5. Prescription image text scan (adults aged 19+ only). For profiles aged 19 or older, the app can use Tesseract.js in the browser to extract printed text from an image. This is OCR, not medical interpretation: it can misread names, numbers, units and abbreviations. It does not calculate a schedule or automatically add medicines. The user must compare the output with the original prescription and ask a doctor or pharmacist about anything unclear. The image is processed in the browser; the OCR software and English language data may be downloaded from a third-party CDN. Do not use the scan if you do not accept that software download. The feature is hidden and blocked for profiles aged 18 and younger.

6. Information and privacy. Profile names, dates of birth, medicine details, reminder settings and taken marks are stored in this browser on this device. The date of birth is used for age checks, birthday reminders and PIN recovery. Anyone with access to this device or browser profile may be able to view them. The OCR text is displayed temporarily and is not intentionally saved in the app's local profile data. Protect the device and do not share screenshots or backups publicly.

7. Sharing and backups. MediMate does not send prescription images or medicine details to a generative AI service. If you choose the Google Calendar icon for a medicine, its name and dose are included in a calendar link handled by Google. A backup is plain text, is not encrypted, and includes profile names, dates of birth and medicine details; keep it private. Clearing browser data or using Reset app deletes local data. Store backups securely.

8. If something goes wrong. If anyone may have taken the wrong medicine, an extra dose or an unknown substance, contact a parent or guardian, doctor, pharmacist or local emergency service promptly. Do not use this app to decide what to do next.

9. Service limits and legal rights. The app is provided as a prototype reminder tool and may contain errors, interruptions or data loss. The developer cannot promise uninterrupted service, perfect accuracy, successful reminders or that every device/browser will behave identically. These terms do not remove or limit any rights or responsibilities that cannot legally be excluded. Obtain legal and regulatory advice before public or commercial release.

10. Agreement. By ticking the agreement box, you confirm these terms have been read. For a profile belonging to someone aged 18 or younger, a parent or legal guardian must confirm supervision during setup. You can delete local profile data at any time with Reset app.
